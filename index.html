Where the numbers come from. Ages and "best with" player counts are BoardGameGeek community polls. Games marked "box age" have no community vote yet.

Play times are BGG's listed range (BGG has no community poll for time). Allow extra time to teach a new game.

Mechanisms are BGG's mechanics, grouped. Weight is BGG's 1–5 weight. The wedge is a third of your shelf, ranked by a weighted BGG rating that pulls games with few ratings toward your shelf's average.

Collection map

Wedges split your shelf into thirds by weighted BGG rating and resize to fit their games. Heavier games (BGG weight) sit closer to the center. Tap a game to edit it.

Pinch to zoom · drag to move

Tap any row in the right-hand panels to filter the map. In Best with, Play time and Kid friendly, a row includes everything below it; tap one of those included rows to cross it out. In categories and mechanisms, tap once to include and twice to exclude (for example, everything except Party). Different panels narrow each other down. Downloads show the filtered map.
